## Data and writer fitting

- **Pretraining data.** The generator checkpoints and Microsoft’s handwritten TrOCR checkpoint use the IAM Handwriting Database. U.-V. Marti and H. Bunke describe it in *The IAM-database: an English sentence database for offline handwriting recognition* (2002). The IAM site provides registration and noncommercial research access. [Dataset paper](https://doi.org/10.1007/s100320200071) · [Dataset provider](https://fki.tic.heia-fr.ch/databases/iam-handwriting-database)
- **Character shapes.** The neural generators use visual archetypes from GNU Unifont. The bundled `unifont.pickle` is the file supplied by DiffBrush. The formula renderer uses separate marks extracted from the project worksheet. [Unifont authors and license terms](https://unifoundry.com/unifont/index.html) · [Bundled font notice](../models/diffbrush/third_party_repo/files/UNIFONT-LICENSE.txt) · [Archetype file in DiffBrush](https://github.com/dailenson/DiffBrush/blob/da9addc1140bdfec463b2d41a974a0a392f80798/files/unifont.pickle)
- **Project samples.** Writer_001’s natural lines are crops of lecture reading notes. The independent test uses a different original notes page. Writer_002 supplies a handwritten pangram. The example transcripts label these specific images. The raw symbol sheet supplies the math glyphs in the proof figures. [Writer_001 source names and digests](../data/style_refs/writer_001/MANIFEST.json) · [Writer_002 pangram](../data/style_refs/writer_002/natural_prose_primary.png) · [Transcripts](../examples/transcripts) · [Raw worksheet](../data/worksheets/writer_001/writer_001_worksheet_01.png)
- **Architecture and loss.** The word model uses a ResNet-18 style encoder, from Kaiming He et al. (2016). The local fit uses Adam, from Diederik Kingma and Jimmy Ba (2015). The shape objective combines a soft Dice overlap term evaluated at three scales with an ink-density penalty. Milletari, Navab and Ahmadi (2016) describe a differentiable Dice objective. [ResNet](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.03385) · [Adam](https://arxiv.org/abs/1412.6980) · [Dice objective](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.04797) · [Handgen fitting implementation](../handgen/vatr/fit.py)

## Recognition, measurement and rendering

- Minghao Li et al. developed **TrOCR**. The optional CPU reader uses Microsoft’s handwritten checkpoint. Apple Vision and Tesseract provide other local OCR routes. The experiment records identify the reader for each region. [TrOCR paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.10282) · [Microsoft checkpoint](https://huggingface.co/microsoft/trocr-base-handwritten/tree/eaacaf452b06415df8f10bb6fad3a4c11e609406) · [Apple Vision](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/vision/recognizing-text-in-images) · [Tesseract](https://tesseract-ocr.github.io/tessdoc/)
- Image preparation uses Nobuyuki Otsu’s threshold-selection method (1979). Pillow and NumPy supply image operations. The separate stroke-thickness experiment used scikit-image skeletonization to measure the ink against the writer’s letter-body height. [Otsu’s paper](https://doi.org/10.1109/TSMC.1979.4310076) · [scikit-image](https://scikit-image.org/docs/stable/api/skimage.morphology.html#skimage.morphology.skeletonize) · [Pillow](https://python-pillow.org/) · [NumPy](https://numpy.org/)
- Neural inference uses PyTorch, Torchvision, Hugging Face Diffusers and Transformers. Writer adapters and converted VATr++ weights use Safetensors. librsvg rasterizes SVG pages, Poppler rasterizes PDF worksheet inputs, and SQLite stores job and asset metadata. [PyTorch MPS](https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/stable/notes/mps.html) · [Torchvision](https://docs.pytorch.org/vision/stable/) · [Diffusers](https://github.com/huggingface/diffusers) · [Transformers](https://github.com/huggingface/transformers) · [Safetensors](https://github.com/huggingface/safetensors) · [librsvg](https://gnome.pages.gitlab.gnome.org/librsvg/) · [Poppler](https://poppler.freedesktop.org/) · [SQLite](https://sqlite.org/)
- One-DM and FW-GAN were considered during model selection. The published experiments use DiffBrush and VATr++. [One-DM](https://github.com/dailenson/One-DM) · [FW-GAN](https://github.com/DAIR-Group/FW-GAN)

## Reproducing and citing an experiment

The model guide gives the checkpoint digests and installation paths. The results file records the train and validation word splits, separate test words, page OCR reads and adapter digests. Include the Handgen commit, the checkpoint revision, the writer split, the OCR reader and the rendering mode when reporting new measurements.
